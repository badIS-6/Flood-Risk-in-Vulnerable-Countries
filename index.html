Methodology & Formulas

1. Normalization (0 to 1):
Every variable is scaled across the global dataset extremes:
N = (Raw - Min) / (Max - Min)

2. Directional Risk:
• Direct scaling: Historical Flood Value (35%), Rainfall (25%), Temperature (5%). Higher raw values yield higher risk.
• Inverse scaling (1 - N): Elevation (15%), Slope (10%), NDVI (10%). Low elevation basins, flat slopes, and low vegetation accelerate pooling and runoff.

3. Score Formula (0 to 100):
Score = [ (0.35 × N_val) + (0.25 × N_rain) + (0.15 × (1 - N_elev)) + (0.10 × (1 - N_slope)) + (0.10 × (1 - N_ndvi)) + (0.05 × N_temp) ] × 100

4. Classifications:
Very Low (0–20) | Low (21–40) | Moderate (41–60) | High (61–80) | Very High (81–100)
